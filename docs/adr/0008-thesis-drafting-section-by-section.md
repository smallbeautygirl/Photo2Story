# Thesis drafting proceeds section-by-section with reasoning sign-off

Status: accepted

The advisor's feedback was that AI-drafted prose reads fluently but its underlying logic is often not genuinely the student's own. Claude drafts one thesis section at a time and pauses for the student to confirm the reasoning is theirs before moving to the next section, rather than generating a full chapter draft in one pass. Drafts are produced as plain text/Markdown for fast iteration, then transferred into the official Word (.docx) format the advisor edits in, once content is settled.

Generating the whole chapter in one pass was rejected: it would reproduce the exact failure mode the advisor flagged, letting reasoning errors compound silently across sections before anyone caught them.
