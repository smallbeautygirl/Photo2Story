# Thesis chapter boundary: Chapter 3 is architecture only; evaluation design and results both live in Chapter 4

Status: accepted (supersedes this ADR's two earlier versions — "architecture only / eval in Ch4", then "architecture + eval design / eval results in Ch4")

The authoritative chapter split is the one already drafted on 2026-08-11 in `docs/thesis/chapter1_motivation.md` and `docs/thesis/chapter3_methodology.md`, informed by the advisor's actual 2026-08-03 feedback: **Chapter 3 (研究方法) covers only the four-stage pipeline architecture and the three ADR-backed design decisions (text-overlay detection, badness heuristic, fixed font)** — no evaluation content of any kind, not even the evaluation method's design. **Chapter 4 covers the ablation experiment design and the full evaluation methodology** (the narrative-continuity taxonomy, its calibration against real published picture books, CLIPScore) **together with the measured results**. Chapter 5 combines results discussion with the thesis's contributions and limitations, rather than being a separate results-only chapter.

This replaces an earlier draft decision (recorded then revised in this same ADR slot) that had put the continuity-evaluation *design* in Chapter 3 on the reasoning that the taxonomy was itself a core contribution. That reasoning no longer holds: the advisor named the two core contributions as (1) context-aware photo selection and (2) cross-page continuity (text-level causal inference, and the planned FLUX Kontext image-conditioning fix for visual/character continuity) — the continuity taxonomy and LLM-judge are the *instrument* used to measure contribution (2), not a contribution in their own right. Keeping Chapter 3 to architecture only also matches how the existing draft is already written, and reopening that boundary now would mean rewriting a chapter that already reflects real advisor sign-off.
